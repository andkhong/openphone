Your apps don’t need to change. OpenMob reads the screen and takes the steps you would, with you in control.

01 /

Connect your phone

Pair your Android with your account. Your own device, with the apps and accounts you already use.

02 /

Ask in your own words

Find a place, play something, or work through a task. Choose a model and tell it what you have in mind.

03 /

Watch it happen

Follow the taps, typing, and progress in a live view. Step in, change direction, or stop at any time.

Choose when OpenMob asks for your approval. Your phone has the final say on sensitive actions.

How permissions work
A phone paired to youYour account connects to your devices, with approval on the phone.
Permission on your termsSet how often to ask. Looser permissions need your approval on the device.
Always a way to stopStop the task from your dashboard or disconnect on your phone.
Who sees your screenScreens are read by Claude (Anthropic) and classified by Jev (TypeSafe) to decide each step. Screenshots aren’t stored.
No passwords, no unlockingOpenMob never types passwords or PINs and can’t unlock your phone.

Get access

OpenMob is in an invite-only beta for Android 11 and newer.

  1. Already invited? Open the one-time sign-in link you were sent. It signs you in and opens your dashboard.
  2. Link expired? Ask whoever invited you for a new one. Links work once and expire after 10 minutes.
  3. Want to join? Ask the person running this OpenMob server for an invite.

Running this server? Create an account with openmob account add you@example.com, then make a sign-in link with openmob account link you@example.com.
