How it works

Works with the apps you already have. Nothing to root, no cable needed.

1

Ask

Speak or type a request in the OpenMob app or on your dashboard, from anywhere.

2

The agent looks

It reads your phone's screen, works out where it is, and decides the next tap, one step at a time.

3

Your phone acts

The app performs each step and you watch it happen live. It tells you when it's done.

You stay in control

Built so that only you can use your phone, and you can stop it any time.

  • Each phone pairs only to its owner's account, approved with the phone's fingerprint or PIN.
  • An always-visible notification while the agent can act, with a one-tap Disconnect.
  • Asks before buying, sending or deleting anything you didn't ask for.
  • Never types passwords and can't unlock your phone.
  • Remove a phone from your account at any time; it's cut off instantly.
  • Screenshots aren't stored; they're only used to decide the next step.

Get access

OpenMob is in an invite-only beta for Android 11 and newer.

  1. Already invited? Open the one-time sign-in link you were sent. It signs you in and opens your dashboard.
  2. Link expired? Ask whoever invited you for a new one. Links work once and expire after 10 minutes.
  3. Want to join? Ask the person running this OpenMob server for an invite.
Running this server? Create an account with openmob account add you@example.com, then make a sign-in link with openmob account link you@example.com.
