Connect a phone

Any Android phone (Android 11+) can join. No cable, no developer mode.

  1. Scan the QR codeWith the phone's camera. It opens a page on the phone.
  2. Install the OpenMob appFirst time only: download it from that page and allow the install.
  3. Tap Open OpenMob, then confirmThe phone asks once, with its fingerprint or PIN. No sign-in on the phone.
  4. Follow the app's checklistTurn on OpenMob in Accessibility, allow notifications and background use. Disconnect any time from the notification.
